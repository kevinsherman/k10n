Tower Kopia Backup

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy describes Tower Kopia Backup, a privately operated application for the owner’s personal backups. It is not offered as a public service.

Data accessed

The application accesses authorized Google Drive files and folders, including backup repository contents and metadata, as permitted by the granted OAuth scope.

Purpose

Google Drive access is used to create, maintain, verify, and restore encrypted personal backups. Google OAuth authorizes Rclone to perform these operations within the granted scope.

Storage and encryption

Kopia encrypts backup contents locally before uploading them to Google Drive through Rclone. Google Drive is the selected storage provider for the encrypted backup repository.

Credentials

OAuth credentials are stored in the private server configuration. They are not published on this website.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared for advertising, or intentionally disclosed to third parties. Google stores the encrypted backups as the selected storage provider.

Retention and deletion

Backup snapshots are managed according to the configured retention policies. The account owner can revoke the application’s OAuth access through their Google Account connections settings.

Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not by itself delete backups already stored in Google Drive. Stored backups can be removed by the account owner.
