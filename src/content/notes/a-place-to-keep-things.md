## A small beginning

I'd like this to be a place for things worth keeping: a useful explanation, a half-formed question, or a story that doesn't need to be new to be interesting.

There isn't a publishing schedule. Some notes might be technical. Others might have nothing to do with computers.

The point is to have somewhere to put them.
